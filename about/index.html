About Little Leaf Kits

Our Story

Little Leaf Kits was born from a simple passion: making the joy of gardening accessible to everyone, regardless of space, experience, or time constraints. Founded in 2020, we believe that everyone deserves to experience the therapeutic benefits of nurturing plants and watching them thrive.

What started as a hobby in a small apartment has grown into a mission to bring the calming presence of succulents into homes, offices, and hearts across the country. We carefully curate each kit to ensure that even first-time plant parents can succeed and fall in love with the rewarding world of plant care.

Our Mission

To democratize the joy of gardening by providing thoughtfully designed succulent kits that empower anyone to create their own green sanctuary, no matter their living situation or experience level.

🌱 Sustainability

We source our plants from ethical growers and use eco-friendly packaging materials whenever possible.

📚 Education

Every kit includes detailed care guides and tips to help you become a confident plant parent.

🎨 Creativity

Our kits are designed to inspire creativity and provide a relaxing, meditative experience.

🤝 Community

We're building a community of plant lovers who support and inspire each other's green journeys.
